# Sample media

`sample_crossing.mp4` is an 8 second re-encode of a U.S. Navy public-domain video:

- Title: Crossing Guard
- DVIDS id: 970643
- Wikimedia Commons: [File:Crossing Guard (970643).webm](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crossing_Guard_(970643).webm)
- Credit: U.S. Navy video by Seaman Jack Pridham
- License: public domain (U.S. Government work)

The clip is scaled to 960×540 and starts 8 seconds into the source. `demo.gif` and `demo_frame.png` are annotated by this repository's detector and tracker. They are not official Navy media.

YOLOv4-tiny weights are not stored here. `scripts/fetch_assets.sh` downloads them from the AlexeyAB darknet release.
